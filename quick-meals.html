Delicious Meals in 15 Minutes

Perfect for busy weeknights, college students, or anyone short on time.

Quick Meal Ideas

  • Stir-fry: Vegetables with protein over rice or noodles
  • Quesadillas: With leftover chicken, cheese, and salsa
  • Pasta Aglio e Olio: Garlic, olive oil, and parmesan
  • Breakfast Burritos: Eggs, cheese, and veggies in a tortilla

Meal Prep Tips

Prep ingredients on weekends. Keep pantry staples stocked. Use pre-cut vegetables to save time.
