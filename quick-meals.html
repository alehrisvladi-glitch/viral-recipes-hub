Quick 15-Minute Meals

Delicious meals in 15 minutes or less! Perfect for busy weeknights and students.

Quick Meal Ideas

  • Stir-Fry: Vegetables with protein (chicken, tofu, or shrimp) over rice
  • Quesadillas: Tortillas with cheese, beans, and leftover chicken
  • Pasta Aglio e Olio: Garlic, olive oil, chili flakes, and parmesan
  • Breakfast Burritos: Eggs, cheese, salsa, and avocado in a tortilla
  • Grilled Cheese & Tomato Soup: Classic comfort food combo

Meal Prep Tips

Prep ingredients on weekends to save time during the week. Keep pantry staples like pasta, rice, and canned goods for quick meals.
